# Austin Deserves Another Kind of Night

What if going out didn’t have to mean finding a bar?

We want more room for young people in Austin to gather without a drink becoming the price of belonging. A place to wander, hear a set, meet an artist, or simply stay awhile.

Nightmarket is our answer in progress: always free to enter, with independent makers, music, food, and enough room for an unexpected conversation. Buying something supports a maker. Buying nothing should never make you feel out of place.

Summer gives this idea its own rhythm. Let the heat soften. Let the lights come on. Give people something to look forward to when the day finally lets go.
