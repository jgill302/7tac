# Jas Gill, After Dark

A founder conversation, shaped by the Nightmarket × 7TAC team. This editorial draft draws on Jas’s supplied background; it is not a recorded interview.

Where does this begin?

Jas Gill brings the spirit of ’90s NYC to Nightmarket.atx: independent, curious, and more interested in who is in the room than who is on the list.

What does architecture have to do with a night out?

For Jas, architecture and technology are part of how a gathering takes shape. The room, the movement through it, and the little details all matter. Friends and close collaborators with sound expertise bring their ears; together, the circle considers lighting, atmosphere, and how a space feels when people arrive.

And the people?

That is where the quiet magic enters. Jas has a way of turning a passing conversation into a welcome—a personable warmth that makes strangers feel a little less like strangers. There is something almost mystical about the ease of it, though the intention is simple: make people feel good.

What are you building toward?

One-off experiences that belong to the people who were actually there. Sound, shopping, unexpected connections. A reason to put the phone away and step into real life. Nightmarket starts small, with the belief that a good room can open up a bigger world.
