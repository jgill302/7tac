# Founder chat, After Dark

By the Nightmarket × 7TAC team.

Before Nightmarket.atx, there was New York: the nightlife, the pop-ups, the feeling that something was happening around the corner and you had to be there to understand it. Jas Gill carries that spirit with him.

His sisters put him onto Triple Five Soul and the creative world of SoHo. That introduction stayed with him—the meeting of clothes, music, street culture, and people making their own scene. The ’90s NYC spirit runs through Nightmarket: independent, resourceful, alive.

Jas brings a background in architecture and technology to the way a night takes shape. Space, light, and movement become part of the experience. Friends and close collaborators with sound expertise help build the atmosphere, curating one-off encounters that belong to the room.

Then there is Jas himself. Personable, curious, and quietly magnetic, he has a way of making a stranger feel like someone the night was waiting for. Call it intuition or a little everyday magic. Around him, conversation tends to become connection.

That is what we are building with Nightmarket: live experiences you had to see in person, like the NYC pop-ups that first caught his imagination. A set that only happened once. An artist you met beside their work. Something you bought from the person who made it. No replay quite captures it.

From New York memories to Austin nights, the invitation is simple: come outside. Something good might happen.
